Radha Jap Privacy Policy

Last Updated: August 1, 2026

Commitment: Radha Jap respects your spiritual journey. We are dedicated to protecting your data with bank-level security and total transparency.

1. Data We Collect

We collect only the minimum information required to provide our services:

  • Name: To personalize your experience and display your rank on the global leaderboard.
  • Email Address: Used strictly for authentication via Firebase and to sync your progress across devices.
  • Spiritual Progress: Your jap counts, goals, and journal entries are stored securely on our cloud servers.

2. Data Security (Encryption)

Your data is stored using Google Firebase. We employ industry-standard encryption protocols (SSL/TLS for data in transit and AES-256 for data at rest). This means your personal realizations and counts are unreadable to unauthorized parties.

3. Zero Third-Party Sharing

We never sell, rent, or trade your data. We do not share your personal information with third-party advertisers or data brokers. Your bhakti is between you and the Divine.

4. Your Rights (Account Deletion)

You have full control over your data. At any time, you can use the "Delete Account" button within the app settings. This action will instantly and permanently erase all your data from our servers. Once deleted, this information cannot be recovered.

5. Free Service and Donations

Radha Jap is currently a 100% free spiritual service. We do not offer any in-app purchases, paid subscriptions, or digital goods. Every feature is unlocked by default for the benefit of the global spiritual community.

  • No Subscriptions: There are no recurring fees or cancellation requirements.
  • Refunds: Since the app is free and does not process payments, the concept of refunds for digital goods does not apply.

6. Contact Us

If you have questions regarding this policy, please contact us at: support@radhajap.com
